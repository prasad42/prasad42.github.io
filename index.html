The questions I keep returning to

My research sits where coherent quantum dynamics meet measurement and loss. These are the questions I am following now.

Q.A

Can collective measurements create a different kind of transition?

In a random quantum circuit, frequent measurements suppress the spread of entanglement. The system crosses from a volume-law phase to an area-law phase. I am asking what changes when those measurements act collectively instead of one site at a time.

The test is whether collective measurement changes the transition's universality class.

Working diagram, not research data

Q.B

What does a driven quantum lattice settle into?

Dissipative bosonic lattices can support bistability and non-equilibrium steady states across the Mott-superfluid regimes. I use the Gutzwiller and SU(3) truncated Wigner approximations to build a theoretical account of that behavior.

The aim is a useful description of driven ultracold quantum matter, including where mean-field intuition fails.

Q.C

Can Clifford dynamics restore a broken U(1) symmetry?

I study whether random U(1)-symmetric Clifford circuits can restore locally broken symmetry while preserving total magnetization. Using entanglement asymmetry, I test whether entanglement alone, without non-stabilizer “magic,” can drive restoration and produce a quantum Mpemba effect.

Preliminary simulations show symmetry restoration for a product state. I am now testing how the result depends on the initial state and circuit structure.

Restricted gate set: phase · controlled-Z · swap

Published result Physical Review E · 2025

Comparative Study of Indicators of Chaos in the Closed and Open Dicke Model

With Aranya Bhattacharyya and B. Prasanna Venkatesh. We compare how familiar signatures of quantum chaos behave when the Dicke model is closed and when it exchanges energy with its surroundings.
